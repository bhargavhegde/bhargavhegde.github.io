## About CAVAS Lab

The **Connected and Autonomous Vehicle Applications and Systems (CAVAS)** Laboratory at the University at Buffalo is at the forefront of research in connected and autonomous vehicle technologies. The lab develops integrated instruments for Connected and Autonomous Vehicle Evaluation and Experimentation (iCAVE2), along with instrumented vehicles including a self-driving shuttle (Olli bus) and UB's Lincoln MKZ AV platform.

### Lab Mission

CAVAS Lab bridges the gap between existing simulators and road testing facilities by providing a flexible, scalable, and safe platform for comprehensive evaluation and experiments of CV/AV technologies. The lab is particularly suitable for answering "what-if" questions related to safety, efficiency, and sustainability arising from human-automation interactions with emerging technologies and rare/extreme events.

### Research Focus Areas

- **Real-time 3D Perception**: LiDAR-based object detection and tracking
- **V2X Communications**: Vehicle-to-Everything cooperative perception
- **Sensor Fusion**: Integration of multiple sensor modalities
- **Autonomous Vehicle Systems**: Development and testing of AV platforms
- **Safety and Validation**: Comprehensive evaluation of CV/AV technologies

## Research Projects

### 🚗 [DAIR-V2X Cooperative 3D Object Detection](/projects/dair-v2x)

**Full reproduction of DAIR-V2X late-fusion model for cooperative 3D object detection in V2X scenarios**

This project implements a complete reproduction of the DAIR-V2X cooperative 3D object detection system, focusing on Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) communication scenarios. The system uses late-fusion techniques to combine LiDAR point cloud data from multiple vehicles for improved perception accuracy.

#### Key Achievements

- ✅ **40.01% AP** on vehicle class detection
- ✅ **37.25% 3D AP@0.7** (8.9% gain over original implementation)
- ✅ **50.51% BEV AP@0.7** (6.7% gain over original)
- ✅ **12.3% reduction** in communication cost (898.10 bytes)

#### Technologies

- **Languages**: Python, Shell
- **Frameworks**: PyTorch
- **Hardware**: NVIDIA RTX 3080 Ti
- **OS**: Ubuntu
- **Tools**: Conda, CUDA
