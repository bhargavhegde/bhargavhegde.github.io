## About CAVAS Lab

The **CAVAS Lab** at the University at Buffalo focuses on connected and autonomous vehicle technologies. The lab develops iCAVE2 (integrated instrument for CV/AV evaluation) and operates instrumented vehicles including a self-driving Olli bus and Lincoln MKZ AV platform.

---

## My Research Work

As a **Research Associate** at CAVAS Lab, I focus on:

- **Real-time 3D perception** using LiDAR point cloud processing
- **Cooperative perception** in V2X scenarios
- **Late-fusion architectures** for multi-vehicle detection

---

## Research Projects

### 🚗 [DAIR-V2X Cooperative 3D Object Detection](/projects/dair-v2x)

Full reproduction of DAIR-V2X late-fusion model for cooperative 3D object detection in V2X scenarios.

**Key Achievements:**
- ✅ **40.01% AP** on vehicle class detection
- ✅ **37.25% 3D AP@0.7** (8.9% improvement)
- ✅ **50.51% BEV AP@0.7** (6.7% improvement)
- ✅ **12.3% reduction** in communication cost

**Technologies:** Python, PyTorch, CUDA, LiDAR

[View Full Project Details →](/projects/dair-v2x)
